# Platform Tone Reference

## 抖音

- Goal: stop scroll, finish watch, trigger comments.
- Hook: direct conflict, mistake, taboo, or quick diagnostic frame.
- Style: short spoken lines, visible action every 5-10 seconds.
- Best CTA: `你是哪种情况？评论区写“宽/假/痕/鼻尖/通气”。`
- Avoid: long background, essay tone, soft opening, over-explaining.

## 小红书

- Goal: search, save, trust, comment.
- Title: keyword first, under 20 Chinese characters when possible.
- Medical main-video structure: `先说结论 -> 适合谁 -> 怎么自查 -> 面诊问什么 -> 收藏提醒`.
- Doctor-life Vlog structure: `场景钩子 -> 观众能看到什么 -> 拍摄观察点 -> 隐私边界 -> 收藏提醒`.
- Tone: experience note, not sales pitch.
- Do not force `面诊前准备` into filming, props, script-writing, or content-review Vlogs.
- For non-medical main videos about content review, production, or data, replace `面诊前准备` with `执行要点`.
- Avoid: exaggerated emojis, fake personal experience, guaranteed outcomes.

## 微博

- Goal: opinion spread and discussion.
- Structure: one quotable opinion + short list + question.
- Tone: concise, sharp, non-aggressive.
- Avoid: long medical detail and heavy hashtags.

## 微信公众号

- Goal: trust asset and searchable long-form explanation.
- Structure: headline, scene, core judgment, 3-5 sections, checklist, caveat.
- Tone: calm, evidence-aware, editorial.
- Avoid: pure short-video style, unexplained claims, hidden ad language.
