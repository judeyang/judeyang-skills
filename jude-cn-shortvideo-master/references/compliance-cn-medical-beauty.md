# CN Medical Beauty Compliance Reference

Use this for doctor IP, medical beauty, plastic surgery, eye/nose repair, anti-aging, and recovery-care content.

## High-Risk Claims

Remove or rewrite:

- `保证 / 一定 / 绝对 / 永久 / 无痕 / 零风险 / 一次修好 / 快速恢复`
- `最厉害 / 第一 / 顶级 / 神手 / 别人做不了我能做`
- direct promises of `自然 / 稳定 / 安全 / 有效 / 恢复快`
- price, quota, booking scarcity, patient volume, cross-city demand as proof
- before/after case proof without confirmed authorization and platform suitability
- viewer diagnosis: `你就是... / 你必须做... / 你适合...`

## Safer Substitutions

Prefer:

- `需要结合基础判断`
- `可能要看皮肤、疤痕、张力和功能`
- `面诊前先准备`
- `这类情况不建议只看模板`
- `常见误区`
- `合并后的典型问题`
- `能不能做，要看时间、组织和风险`

## Patient Privacy

Do not show:

- face, voice, scar details that identify a patient
- medical records, forms, phone numbers, payment records, chat screenshots
- exact travel route or personal story unless authorized and still necessary

For scripts, use blank props:

- `空白资料夹`
- `示意图`
- `模型`
- `打码地图`
- `合成案例卡`

## Platform Safety

Avoid:

- appearance shaming
- insulting aesthetic labels
- gender confrontation
- fear-driven medical conversion
- direct appointment conversion inside educational content

Use:

- risk education
- decision checklist
- preparation checklist
- comment classification
