# Transcript Adaptation Rules

## What To Preserve

Preserve the source video's:

- usable original lines
- opening function
- sentence length pattern
- beat order
- scene type
- tension mechanism
- CTA mechanism
- rough duration class

## When To Change

Change or remove source wording only when it creates:

- medical compliance risk
- platform moderation risk
- privacy or patient-identification risk
- inaccurate target-account facts
- poor oral rhythm or excessive length
- weak hook, weak CTA, or low shootability

## Duration Classes

- `超短`: 10-20s
- `短切`: 20-45s
- `常规`: 45-75s
- `长口播`: 75-120s
- `Vlog`: variable, scene-led

First-pass起号 should prefer `超短` or `短切` unless the benchmark is clearly Vlog.

## Rewrite Test

A rewrite passes only if:

1. It keeps the source's useful lines, shape, rhythm, and content intent.
2. It sounds like the target account can say it naturally.
3. It can be shot with available people, props, and locations.
4. Compliance risk is controlled.
5. Platform fit is stronger than the raw source.
